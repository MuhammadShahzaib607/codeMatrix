What the studio makes

Originals, editions and commissions

Three ways to work with Shahzaib.art, each with a fixed process, a written timeline and a price agreed before the first brush touches the canvas.

Original canvas paintings

Hand-painted oil and acrylic works on stretched linen, each one a single piece signed by Shahzaib. Studio works ship stretched, varnished and ready to hang, with a certificate of authenticity and a lifetime care sheet.

Limited-edition prints

Museum-grade giclee editions of the most requested works, limited to sixty signed and numbered copies on 310gsm cotton rag. Every order includes a hand-numbered certificate and archival packaging built to survive the post.

Bespoke commissions

A private commission starts with a colour and story conversation, moves through two studio sketches you approve, and finishes as a piece sized for the wall it was made for. Typical turnaround is six to eight weeks.
