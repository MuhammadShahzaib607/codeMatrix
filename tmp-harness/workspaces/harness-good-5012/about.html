About — Shahzaib.art studio, Lahore

About the studio — painting in Lahore since 2015

Who paints the work, how the studio started, and the process every commission follows from the first sketch to the crate on the truck. Eleven years, one workshop in Gulberg, and more than a hundred works now living in private and public collections.

Since 2015

Eleven years of painting in Lahore

  1. 2015

    A single studio room

    Shahzaib left a commercial illustration job and rented one room in Gulberg with north light and a borrowed easel. The first six canvases sold to friends of friends.

  2. 2018

    First hotel commission

    A boutique hotel in Islamabad asked for nine works for its lobby and corridors. That project set the studio process still used today: colour studies first, client approval, then paint.

  3. 2022

    Editions and worldwide shipping

    The studio began releasing signed giclee editions of sixty, which opened the work to collectors who wanted the colour without the original scale. Shipping now reaches thirty-one countries.

  4. 2026

    The archive opens

    Every work placed since 2015 is now recorded in a digital archive with its provenance, so a piece can be traced and re-valued decades after it left the studio.

The people in the room

Three people make every commission happen, from the first sketch to the crate on the truck.

SQ

Shahzaib Qureshi

Painter and founder

Trained in miniature painting at NCA before moving to large-scale oil work. Paints every original and approves every edition by hand.

MA

Mariam Aslam

Studio manager

Runs commissions end to end: briefs, timelines, framing decisions and the shipping paperwork your customs broker will ask for.

BZ

Bilal Zafar

Editions and printmaking

Calibrates every giclee run against the original canvas so the printed colour holds up beside the painting it came from.
